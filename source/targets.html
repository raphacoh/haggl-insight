Coupon and deal sites own the moment a shopper asks “is there a discount?”, which agents now ask on every purchase. Loyalty and rewards apps are installed inside thousands of merchants’ stores, where the offers are set. One of each gives haggl.ai both sides of the network on day one.

CouponFollow: System1 (NYSE: SST) paid up to $115M for it in 2022; System1 as a whole is now worth about $14M. Traffic was 7.5M visits in the three months to September 2026, down 22.5%. Acquisition

DesiDime: 3.8M visits in the three months to September 2026; price estimated at 1–2× revenue. Its MCP server

Hi Mami: founder Maayan Adam; hundreds of brands; an MCP server that shows its deals inside Claude. MCP listing

Savings.com: owned by Centerfield; 0.4M visits in the three months to September 2026, down 24.9%. Background

Gameball: raised a $3.5M seed in February 2023 (500 Global, P1 Ventures, Launch Africa) and reported 7,000+ businesses; no later round found. Seed round · Shopify app

ReferralCandy: founded 2010 by Dinesh Raju and Zach Cheng; “nearly 2,000” brands and 1,480+ Shopify reviews. Shopify app

Stamped: Tiny (formerly WeCommerce) bought it in April 2021 for up to US$110M. Tiny’s FY2025 results (March 30, 2026) include $37.0M of impairments, with goodwill in its Software & Apps segment. Acquisition · Tiny FY2025

Larger options: Picodi (12,000+ stores in 40+ countries; owner Méliuz is becoming a bitcoin-treasury company; ~$24M) and SimplyCodes (300,000+ stores, had a ChatGPT plugin, plans an agent API; ~$32M).

Loyalty clubs that failed in the browser era: Cashrewards (Australia) shut down on September 8, 2025, after ANZ’s venture arm spent about A$100M; Drop (Toronto, ~US$70M raised) stopped working in 2026. Their retailer contracts may be available for very little. Cashrewards · Drop
